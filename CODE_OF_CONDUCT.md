# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming to everyone, whatever their experience, background or instrument.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully and credit other people's contributions.
- Help beginners. Everyone started somewhere.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and may ban people who break these rules. Report problems by opening a private security advisory on the repository or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
